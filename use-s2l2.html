This tool is not a mental health screening, diagnostic, or therapeutic instrument. It does not assess, diagnose, or treat anxiety, depression, trauma, or any other mental health condition. It's a personal worksheet — a reflective vocabulary drawn from Vedantic philosophy, Adi Shankara's Bhaja Govindam and the Bhagavad Gita — not a clinical or psychological instrument.

Some of what this ladder names — craving, anger, delusion, memory loss, ruin — can resemble language used to describe emotional or mental health struggles. That resemblance comes from the source texts themselves, not from any claim that this tool can identify, measure, or address those struggles.

Do not use this tool to make decisions about your mental health care, to self-diagnose, or as a substitute for speaking with a licensed therapist, counselor, or physician.

If you are in crisis, or having thoughts of suicide or self-harm, please stop and reach out for support right now:
  • US: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line)
  • Outside the US: contact your local emergency number, or visit findahelpline.com
