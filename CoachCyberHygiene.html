Systems Inventory
2Step 2 of 5 — Know Your Exposure
One note per system your coaching business actually uses. Not a security audit — just a working list so you know what you're running, what it touches, and what to keep an eye on. Saved locally, just like everything else.
Why this matters: the practice #6 in your AI Breach checklist and #2 in Cyber Hygiene both start the same way — you can't secure or govern a system you never wrote down. This tab is where that list actually lives.
Data Inventory
2Step 2 of 5 — Know Your Exposure
Not your tools — the actual sensitive data itself. What do you hold, and where does it live? This is the question Systems Inventory doesn't ask, and it's the one that matters most if something ever goes wrong.
Why this is separate from Systems: "I use Google Drive" tells you nothing about what's actually sitting inside it. Data Inventory is where you name the sensitive thing itself — client notes, payment details, health information — and where it lives, independent of which tool holds it.
